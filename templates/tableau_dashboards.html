A closer look at the type of dashboards I built for VanWonen.

Once the data was structured and stored in our analytical databases, the next step was to make it easy for people to use. Instead of going through hundreds of Excel files and documents for hours, employees could open a dashboard and compare projects, phases and numbers in seconds, and explore the data themselves.

I never connected Tableau directly to the source platforms. Their data structures were made to run software, not for analysis. Every dashboard reads from the databases I prepared with Python, so the data is already shaped for what people need to see.

The dashboards covered many different types of data: business cases from Reasult, budgets and real costs from Microsoft Business Central, acquisition leads from our GIS system, customer satisfaction from our CRM, and even how often people used the different tools in our TechPortal.

In the end, all these dashboards were brought together in a data portal inside our TechPortal, where every employee could find the dashboards relevant to their work, in one place.
