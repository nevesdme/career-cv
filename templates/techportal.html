A closer look at the TechPortal and the digital tools we developed for VanWonen.

The TechPortal was initially created to centralize all our data and tools in one place, so it would be easier for employees to find and use them. We used the normal Microsoft 365 login, so people only had to log in once, making it an even more seamless experience.

In the TechPortal, employees could consult all the company dashboards, built on very different data sources, and use the tools they needed in their daily work, all in the same place. Tools like an enterprise closed AI agent, a GIS system used for acquisition leads, an AI knowledge sharer with information about how the company's projects are structured and how they work, an internal secure file transfer system, and a couple of other tools.

I also helped design and develop a new tool that allowed our financial controllers and project managers to follow how their projects were doing every quarter. It combined data from the different systems we used, like the ERP, the real estate software and the tool's own data. In the end, it was possible to see how each project was developing, the status of its financial data, the important dates of the project, and follow the direction the project was going, while comparing it with its different stages.
